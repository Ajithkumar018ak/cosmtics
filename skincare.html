A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered gently.

A little clarity can make choosing your skincare essentials feel easier.

How do I start a skincare routine?

Start with a gentle cleanser, a moisturiser suitable for your skin and broad-spectrum sunscreen during the daytime. Add other products gradually according to your skin's needs.

How can I choose products for my skin?

Consider whether your skin is dry, oily, combination or sensitive, along with your individual concerns. Check product ingredients and directions before use.

Can I use multiple skincare products together?

Some products work well together, while certain combinations may irritate your skin. Introduce products one at a time and follow their instructions.

How long should I try a new product?

Results vary depending on the product and skin concern. Follow the product directions, use it consistently when appropriate and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

What if my skin feels irritated?

Stop using the suspected product. If irritation is severe, persistent or worsening, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional or dermatologist.
